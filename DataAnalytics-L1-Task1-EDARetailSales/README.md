# Retail Sales Data — Exploratory Data Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to perform **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)** on a retail sales dataset to uncover sales patterns, customer behaviour trends, product performance, and actionable business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

The main objectives of this project are:

- Inspect and understand the structure of the dataset.
- Check dataset shape, column data types, and missing values.
- Calculate mean, median, mode, and standard deviation for numerical variables.
- Analyze monthly and quarterly sales trends.
- Analyze customer age-group and gender distribution.
- Identify the top 10 best-selling products.
- Analyze revenue generated by product categories.
- Examine correlations between numerical variables.
- Create an additional visualization to identify a non-obvious insight.
- Document observations after each visualization.
- Provide actionable business recommendations based on the analysis.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset used for this project is a **Retail Sales Dataset** sourced from **Kaggle**.

The dataset contains retail transaction-related information that can be used to analyze:

- Customer demographics
- Product information
- Sales and revenue
- Quantity sold
- Transaction dates
- Product categories

The dataset was prepared and analyzed using Python before performing the exploratory analysis.

---

## 🔍 Exploratory Data Analysis

### 1. Dataset Inspection

The dataset was initially inspected to understand its structure and quality.

The following checks were performed:

- Number of rows and columns
- Column names
- Data types
- Missing values
- Duplicate records
- Basic information about the dataset

### 2. Descriptive Statistics

Descriptive statistics were calculated for numerical variables, including:

- Mean
- Median
- Mode
- Standard deviation
- Minimum and maximum values
- Quartiles

This helped understand the central tendency, spread, and distribution of the numerical data.

### 3. Monthly Sales Trend

A line chart was created to analyze sales performance across different months.

This analysis helps identify:

- High-performing months
- Low-performing months
- Seasonal patterns
- Changes in customer demand

### 4. Quarterly Sales Trend

Sales were aggregated by quarter and visualized using a line chart.

Quarterly analysis provides a broader view of business performance and helps identify strong and weak periods throughout the year.

### 5. Customer Demographics

Customer demographics were analyzed using age groups and gender.

#### Age Group Analysis

Customers were grouped into suitable age categories to understand the distribution of the customer base.

#### Gender Analysis

A visualization was created to compare the number of customers or transactions across genders.

### 6. Top 10 Best-Selling Products

Products were ranked based on their sales quantity/performance.

A bar chart was used to identify the **top 10 best-selling products**.

This helps determine which products have the highest customer demand.

### 7. Revenue by Product Category

Revenue was aggregated by product category and displayed using a bar chart.

This analysis helps identify which categories contribute the most to overall revenue.

### 8. Correlation Heatmap

A correlation matrix was created for numerical variables and visualized using a Seaborn heatmap.

The heatmap helps identify positive and negative relationships between variables such as:

- Sales
- Revenue
- Quantity
- Customer age
- Other numerical variables available in the dataset

### 9. Additional Visualization

An additional visualization was created to uncover a **non-obvious pattern** in customer behaviour or sales performance.

This analysis goes beyond the required visualizations and provides an additional perspective on the dataset.

The EDA provides insights into:

- Customer purchasing behaviour

- Relationships between numerical variables
- Additional patterns identified through visualization

## 🚀 Business Recommendations

Based on the findings from the analysis, the following recommendations can be considered:

### 1. Inventory Optimization

Maintain sufficient inventory for consistently high-performing products and plan stock levels according to observed sales trends.

### 2. Targeted Marketing

Use customer demographic information to design targeted marketing campaigns for the most relevant customer segments.

### 3. Product Promotion

Promote best-selling products through offers, bundles, and cross-selling strategies to maximize their revenue potential.

### 4. Seasonal Planning

Use monthly and quarterly sales patterns to plan promotional campaigns and inventory requirements before high-demand periods.

### 5. Category Performance Monitoring

Monitor high- and low-revenue product categories and develop strategies to improve the performance of weaker categories.
